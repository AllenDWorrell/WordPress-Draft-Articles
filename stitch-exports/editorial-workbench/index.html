FROM THE OFFICE OF THE PROVOST

The Nova Southeastern University community mourns the passing of Dr. Arthur Sterling, emeritus professor at the Halmos College of Arts and Sciences, whose pioneering research on deep-sea carbonate reefs spanned over three decades.

A memorial reception will be held at the Guy Harvey Oceanographic Center later this month.
